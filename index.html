The Colley Matrix ranks teams from wins and losses alone — a real network of who's beaten whom, nothing else. It never sees margin of victory, injuries, or a team's reputation; a 1-point win counts exactly the same as a 40-point blowout. Strength of schedule isn't guessed at or assigned by anyone — it emerges purely from how each team's results connect to everyone else's, and that network only gets stronger as more real games are played. Early in a season, with few connections between teams, it can disagree with the human poll a lot — that's the method being honest about how little evidence it has yet, not a flaw to explain away.
